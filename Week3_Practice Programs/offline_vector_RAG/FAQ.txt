[FAQ 001: Luggage Policy]
Our custom travel packages allow a maximum of 1 checked bag (up to 23kg/50lbs) and 1 personal item (like a backpack or laptop bag) per traveler. Extra or overweight bags will incur a fee of $50 USD per flight segment, payable directly at the airport check-in counter.

[FAQ 002: Cancellation and Refund Window]
Trips booked through this application can be fully refunded to the original payment method if canceled at least 14 days before the departure date. Cancellations made inside the 14-day window are non-refundable but will automatically receive a 50% travel credit voucher valid for 12 months.

[FAQ 003: Hidden Gem - Paris Secret Spot]
When visiting Paris, our agency highly recommends visiting the 'Musée de la Vie Romantique' located at the foot of Montmartre. It features a beautiful, quiet courtyard garden and a lovely greenhouse tea room that remains completely missed by standard tourist crowds.

[FAQ 004: Emergency Contact Information]
For 24/7 on-trip assistance regarding flight delays, hotel booking issues, or medical emergencies, call our global support line at +1-800-555-0199 or email urgent@aitripplanner.com. Always quote your 6-digit booking reference code.

[FAQ 005: Passport and Visa Validity]
Travelers are entirely responsible for ensuring their passports are valid for at least 6 months beyond their planned return date. For international packages to Japan and the European Schengen zone, visas must be secured at least 30 days prior to departure.

[FAQ 006: Hidden Gem - Tokyo Secret Spot]
For an unforgettable view of Tokyo without the massive lines of Shibuya Sky, head to the Hokutopia Observatory in Kita City. It is completely free to enter and offers a stunning, unobstructed view of the Tokyo Shinkansen bullet trains passing right below.

[FAQ 007: Travel Insurance Coverage]
Our premium tier packages automatically include basic medical travel insurance covering up to $50,000 USD in emergency medical expenses. This basic coverage does not include trip cancellation protection or electronics theft insurance, which must be purchased separately.

[FAQ 008: Check-in and Check-out Times]
Standard hotel partners across all destinations enforce a check-in time of 3:00 PM and a check-out time of 11:00 AM local time. Early check-in requests can be submitted through our app but are subject to room availability upon arrival.

[FAQ 009: Hidden Gem - Rome Secret Spot]
Skip the crowded Trevi Fountain lines in the afternoon and visit the Aventine Keyhole on Aventine Hill. Looking through this brass keyhole provides a perfectly framed, magical view of St. Peter’s Basilica dome through a tunnel of manicured garden hedges.

[FAQ 010: Tour Guide Gratuity Guidelines]
While tips are completely optional, it is customary to tip local tour guides $10 to $20 USD per day for exceptional service, and private drivers $5 to $10 USD per day. In countries like Japan, tipping is not practiced and can be considered offensive.
