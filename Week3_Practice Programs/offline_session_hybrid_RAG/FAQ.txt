=== SECTION 1: GLOBAL AGENCY TRAVEL POLICIES ===
[Policy: Luggage Rules]
Our custom travel packages allow a maximum of 1 checked bag (up to 23kg/50lbs) and 1 personal item (like a backpack) per traveler. Extra or overweight bags incur a flat fee of $50 USD per flight segment.

[Policy: Cancellations and Refunds]
Trips booked through this application can be fully refunded if canceled at least 14 days before the departure date. Cancellations made inside the 14-day window are non-refundable but automatically receive a 50% travel credit voucher valid for 12 months.

[Policy: Insurance Coverage]
Our premium tier packages automatically include basic medical travel insurance covering up to $50,000 USD in emergency medical expenses. This does not cover electronics theft or trip delays.

=== SECTION 2: DESTINATION KNOWLEDGE NETWORKS ===
The travel agency organizes custom vacation packages to top global destinations. 

Paris features a hidden gem called Musée de la Vie Romantique, which is located at the foot of Montmartre. Montmartre is a famous hill known for its artistic history.

Tokyo offers an alternative panoramic view from the Hokutopia Observatory, which is situated in Kita City. Kita City allows tourists to view passing Shinkansen bullet trains.

Rome contains a secret spot named the Aventine Keyhole, which is built on Aventine Hill. Aventine Hill offers a perfectly framed view of St. Peter’s Basilica dome.
